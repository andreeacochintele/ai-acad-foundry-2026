---
title: Hardship restructuring options
product: mortgages
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-15
version: 1
---

Customers who anticipate difficulty making their mortgage payments — due to
job loss, illness, or another documented hardship — may request one of the
following restructuring options before missing a payment.

**Payment holiday.** Up to 3 consecutive monthly installments may be
deferred, with the deferred amount added to the end of the loan term. Only
one payment holiday is permitted per 5 years of the loan's life. Interest
continues to accrue on the outstanding balance during the holiday.

**Term extension.** The remaining loan term may be extended by up to 5
years, reducing the monthly installment, provided the customer's age at the
new maturity date does not exceed 75.

**Temporary rate reduction.** For documented cases of job loss (with proof
of unemployment registration), the bank may offer a temporary reduction of
up to 1 percentage point off the current rate for up to 12 months, after
which the rate reverts to the contractual formula.

These options require a written application with supporting documents and
are evaluated case by case; approval is not automatic. They are only
available if requested before an installment becomes overdue — once a
payment is missed, the late payment penalties document governs instead.
