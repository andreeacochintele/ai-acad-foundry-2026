The following documents are required for a mortgage application at Moch
Bank. Requirements differ slightly for employed and self-employed applicants.

**All applicants**
- Valid national ID or passport.
- Proof of address dated within the last 3 months (utility bill or similar).
- Bank statements for the last 3 months, for the account the installment
  will be paid from.

**Employed applicants**
- Employment contract or an employer's letter confirming position and
  salary.
- Payslips for the last 3 months.

**Self-employed applicants**
- Tax returns for the last 2 fiscal years.
- Company registration documents, if applying through a company structure.
- A accountant-certified income statement for the current fiscal year to
  date.

**Property-related, submitted once a property is identified**
- Preliminary sale-purchase agreement or reservation contract.
- Land Registry extract (extras de carte funciară) no older than 30 days.
- Energy performance certificate, where applicable to the property type.

Missing any document from the applicable list will pause the application at
step 2 of the application process until it is provided.
