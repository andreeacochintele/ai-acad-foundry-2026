---
title: Early repayment fee schedule (2026)
product: mortgages
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-15
version: 2
---

This schedule applies to all mortgages originated from 2026-01-15 onward, and
replaces the 2025 fee schedule for those loans. Loans originated before this
date continue to follow the 2025 schedule unless the customer signs an
addendum adopting the new terms.

Early repayment is possible at any time, in full or in part.

During the fixed-rate period, an early repayment fee of **1 percent** of the
amount being repaid early applies, with no threshold — the fee applies from
the first euro of prepayment, unlike the 2025 schedule.

During the variable-rate period, early repayment is free of charge, with no
fee and no limit, the same as under the 2025 terms.

As with the 2025 schedule, partial prepayments shorten the loan term by
default; the customer may request a lower monthly installment instead by
submitting a written request to the mortgage servicing team.
