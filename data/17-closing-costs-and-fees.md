Beyond the interest rate, a Moch Bank mortgage carries the following one-time
costs. These are separate from the early repayment fee schedule, which applies
only when repaying the loan ahead of schedule.

**Origination fee (bank).** 0.5 percent of the approved loan amount, with a
minimum of 200 EUR and a maximum of 1,500 EUR. Deducted from the disbursed
amount at step 9 of the application process (disbursement), not paid
upfront.

**Property appraisal fee (appraiser, not the bank).** Paid directly to the
independent appraiser at step 5 of the application process, typically
150–300 EUR depending on property size and type. Moch Bank does not collect
or mark up this fee.

**Land Registry mortgage lien registration fee.** A flat 60 EUR, paid to the
Land Registry (not the bank) when the mortgage lien is registered at step 8.

**Notary fee for the sale and mortgage deed.** Paid directly to a notary
public of the customer's choosing, not set or capped by the bank. This
typically runs 0.5–1 percent of the property's purchase price, depending on
the notary and the property's registered value.

**What is not a closing cost.** Property and life insurance premiums (see the
mortgage insurance requirements document) are recurring costs, not one-time
closing costs, and are not included in the figures above.
