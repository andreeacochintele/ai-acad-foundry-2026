**IRCC** — the Reference Index for Consumer Credit, published by the
National Bank of Romania, used as the base for variable mortgage rates.

**LTV (Loan-to-Value)** — the loan amount expressed as a percentage of the
property's appraised value. A lower LTV generally qualifies for a lower
interest rate.

**Debt-service-to-income ratio** — the percentage of an applicant's net
monthly income that goes toward all loan installments combined, including
the new mortgage.

**Conditional approval** — a written offer stating the approved loan amount,
rate, and term, valid for a limited period (60 days at Libra Bank), pending
final legal and title checks.

**Amortization** — the gradual repayment of a loan's principal over time
through scheduled installments.

**Acceleration** — the bank's right, after a serious payment default, to
demand immediate repayment of the entire outstanding loan balance rather
than only the missed installments.

**Land Registry lien** — a legal record, registered against the property,
that gives the bank a claim on the property until the mortgage is fully
repaid.
