---
title: Mortgage top-up (additional borrowing on an existing mortgage)
product: mortgages
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-15
version: 1
---

A top-up lets an existing Moch Bank mortgage customer borrow an additional
amount secured against the same property, without repaying or restructuring
the original loan. This is distinct from refinancing, which moves or replaces
the existing loan itself — a top-up sits alongside it as a second tranche.

**Eligibility.** The original mortgage must have had at least 12 consecutive
months of on-time payments, with no late-payment event in that period (see
the late payment penalties document for what counts as late). The combined
loan-to-value of the original mortgage plus the top-up may not exceed 80
percent of the property's current appraised value, which requires a fresh
appraisal at the customer's cost.

**Amount and rate.** The minimum top-up is 5,000 EUR; the maximum is the
lesser of 100,000 EUR or the LTV headroom described above. The top-up tranche
is priced at the loan-to-value rate table's tier for the combined LTV, not
the original loan's rate — meaning a top-up can carry a different rate than
the mortgage it sits alongside.

**Income re-check.** The debt-service-to-income calculation is redone for the
combined installment (original mortgage plus top-up) against current income,
using the same 40 percent limit as a new application. A top-up is not
approved solely on the strength of the original approval.

**Term.** The top-up's term cannot exceed the remaining term of the original
mortgage.
