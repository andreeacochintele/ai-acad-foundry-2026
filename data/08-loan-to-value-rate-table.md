The starting fixed-period interest rate depends on the loan-to-value (LTV)
ratio — the loan amount as a percentage of the property's appraised value. A
lower LTV (a larger down payment) qualifies for a lower rate.

| LTV tier | Down payment | Starting fixed rate (5-year period) | Starting fixed rate (10-year period) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to 60% LTV | 40% or more | 6.20% | 6.80% |
| 60.01–75% LTV | 25–39.99% | 6.45% | 7.05% |
| 75.01–85% LTV | 15–24.99% | 6.75% | 7.35% |
| 85.01–90% LTV | 10–14.99% (First-Time Buyer only) | 7.10% | 7.70% |

Rates above 85% LTV are only available under the First-Time Buyer Mortgage;
the Standard Mortgage requires a minimum 15 percent down payment and
therefore does not go above 85% LTV.

These starting rates apply to the fixed period only. Once the fixed period
ends, all tiers move to the same variable-rate formula: IRCC plus the margin
fixed at origination, as described in the interest rate structure document.

Rates in this table are reviewed quarterly and may change for new
applications; an approved conditional approval letter locks in the rate
shown at the time of approval for 60 days.
