The First-Time Buyer Mortgage is a variant of the Standard Mortgage designed
for applicants purchasing their first home, with a reduced down payment
requirement.

**Definition of first-time buyer.** An applicant qualifies as a first-time
buyer if they have never held title to a residential property, in Romania or
abroad, either individually or jointly. A co-borrower who has previously
owned property disqualifies the application from this program, even if the
primary applicant has not.

**Reduced down payment.** Where the Standard Mortgage requires a minimum 15
percent down payment for a first home, the First-Time Buyer Mortgage reduces
this to **10 percent**, provided the property's appraised value does not
exceed 150,000 EUR.

**Additional conditions.** The applicant must be under 35 years old at
application, and the property must be intended as the applicant's primary
residence for at least 5 years — renting it out within that period requires
converting to a Standard Mortgage at the then-current rate.

**Everything else follows the Standard Mortgage.** Eligibility criteria not
mentioned here (income, employment, credit history), the interest rate
structure, and the early repayment fee schedule are all identical to the
Standard Mortgage; see those documents for full detail. This document
overrides only the down payment percentage and adds the age and residency
conditions above.
