---
title: Green mortgage discount for energy-efficient properties
product: mortgages
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-15
version: 1
---

Libra Bank applies a rate discount to mortgages on properties with a strong
energy performance certificate (EPC), on any of the Standard, First-Time
Buyer, or Refinancing Mortgage products.

**Qualifying rating.** The property must hold a valid EPC of class A or B (on
the national A–G scale) issued by an accredited energy auditor, dated within
the last 5 years, and submitted at application alongside the other required
documents.

**Discount.** A 0.15 percentage point reduction is applied to the starting
fixed-period rate that would otherwise apply from the loan-to-value rate
table, for both the initial fixed period and any subsequent fixed period
chosen at rate lock. The discount does not change the variable-period margin.

**New-build exception.** For a property under construction (see the new-build
staged-drawdown document), a provisional EPC based on the approved building
design is accepted at application, but the discount is reversed — with
interest, applied retroactively — if the final "as-built" EPC after
completion does not confirm class A or B.

**Not combinable.** The green mortgage discount cannot be combined with the
First-Time Buyer Mortgage's reduced down payment being treated as a further
rate reduction; the two benefits stack independently (down payment and rate),
but neither increases the other.

**Re-verification.** The discount is not re-verified after origination; a
property that is later renovated in a way that would lower its energy class
does not lose the discount retroactively.
