---
title: Mortgage eligibility for non-resident and foreign national borrowers
product: mortgages
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-15
version: 1
---

Moch Bank lends to foreign nationals and Romanian citizens who are tax
resident abroad, under the Standard Mortgage product, with the additional
conditions below layered on top of the standard eligibility criteria.

**Down payment.** A non-resident applicant's minimum down payment is 30
percent of the property's appraised value, regardless of whether the property
will be a primary residence, second home, or rental — the first-home and
second-home tiers in the eligibility criteria document do not apply to
non-residents.

**Income and currency.** Income earned and paid in a currency other than EUR
or RON is converted using the European Central Bank reference rate on the
day of application, and a 10 percent haircut is applied to the converted
amount before it enters the debt-service-to-income calculation, to buffer
exchange-rate movement over the loan term.

**Additional documents.** Beyond the standard required documents checklist, a
non-resident applicant must provide a tax residency certificate from their
country of residence, and a source-of-funds declaration for the down payment,
which the bank's compliance team must clear before step 6 (conditional
approval) can be issued.

**Not eligible.** The First-Time Buyer Mortgage and the green mortgage
discount are both unavailable to non-resident applicants; a non-resident
applicant may still apply for a buy-to-let mortgage under that product's
own rules.

**Power of attorney.** A non-resident applicant who cannot sign in person at
contract signing (step 8) may use a notarized power of attorney, apostilled
or legalized as required by the country of issue.
