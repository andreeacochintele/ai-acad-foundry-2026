---
title: Mortgage insurance requirements
product: mortgages
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-15
version: 1
---

Two insurance policies are mandatory for the life of a Libra Bank mortgage;
a third is optional.

**Property insurance (mandatory).** The property must be insured against
fire, flood, and structural damage for at least the outstanding loan amount,
with Libra Bank named as loss payee. The customer may use any insurer; the
bank does not require its own policy be used.

**Life insurance (mandatory).** The primary borrower must hold a life
insurance policy covering at least the outstanding loan balance, decreasing
in line with the amortization schedule. If a co-borrower is present, either
borrower's policy may satisfy this requirement, or both may be insured for a
proportional share.

**Payment protection insurance (optional).** Covers monthly installments for
up to 12 months in the event of involuntary unemployment or temporary
disability. This is not required for loan approval and can be added or
cancelled at any time without affecting the mortgage terms.

Failure to maintain the two mandatory policies is a breach of the mortgage
contract and may result in the bank purchasing forced-placed insurance on
the customer's behalf, at a cost added to the monthly installment.
