Applying for a Libra Bank mortgage follows nine steps, in order. Skipping
ahead is not possible — each step's output is required as input for the next.

1. **Pre-qualification.** The customer completes an online or in-branch
   questionnaire covering income, existing debt, and the target property
   price. The bank returns an estimated maximum loan amount within minutes.
   This estimate is not binding.

2. **Document submission.** The customer submits the documents listed in the
   required documents checklist: identity, income proof, employment proof,
   and property details if already identified.

3. **Credit check.** The bank queries the Central Credit Register and the
   applicant's credit history. This step takes 1 to 2 business days.

4. **Income verification.** A loan officer verifies payslips or tax returns
   against the declared income. Self-employed applicants may be asked for
   additional bank statements covering the past 12 months.

5. **Property appraisal.** An independent appraiser, selected from the bank's
   approved list, values the property. The applicant pays the appraisal fee
   directly to the appraiser, not to the bank.

6. **Conditional approval.** If steps 3 through 5 are satisfactory, the bank
   issues a conditional approval letter stating the approved amount, rate,
   and term, valid for 60 days.

7. **Legal and title check.** The bank's legal department verifies the
   property has clear title and no undisclosed liens.

8. **Contract signing.** The customer and the bank sign the mortgage
   contract and the property is registered with a mortgage lien in the
   customer's name at the Land Registry.

9. **Disbursement.** Funds are released to the seller (for a purchase) or to
   the customer's account (for refinancing) within 5 business days of
   signing, once the Land Registry confirms the lien registration.

The full process, from pre-qualification to disbursement, typically takes 4
to 6 weeks, assuming no delays at the credit check or legal review stages.
