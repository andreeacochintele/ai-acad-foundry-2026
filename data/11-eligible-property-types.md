---
title: Eligible property types
product: mortgages
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-15
version: 1
---

Moch Bank finances the following property types under its mortgage
products: apartments in blocks of flats, individual houses, duplexes, and
land with an approved building permit for self-build projects.

**Not eligible.** The bank does not finance agricultural land without a
building permit, commercial-only properties (offices, retail units) under
the retail mortgage products, properties under active legal dispute, or
properties located in a flood-risk zone classified as high risk by the
national flood risk map, unless flood insurance at replacement value is
already in place at application.

**Self-build projects.** Land with a building permit is financed in
tranches, released as construction milestones are verified by the bank's
appraiser, rather than as a single disbursement.

**Mixed-use buildings.** A residential unit within a mixed-use building
(residential above, commercial ground floor) is eligible, provided the unit
itself is zoned residential.
