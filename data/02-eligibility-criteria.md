To qualify for a Standard Mortgage at Libra Bank, an applicant must meet all of
the following conditions.

**Age.** The applicant must be at least 21 years old at application, and the
loan must be fully repaid before the applicant turns 70.

**Down payment.** A minimum down payment of 15 percent of the property's
appraised value is required for a first home. For a second home or a rental
property, the minimum down payment is 25 percent.

**Income.** The applicant's total monthly loan installments across all loans
(including the new mortgage) must not exceed 40 percent of net monthly income,
in line with the National Bank of Romania's debt-service-to-income guidance.

**Employment.** The applicant must show at least 12 months of continuous
employment or documented self-employment income, verified through payslips,
an employment contract, or the last two years of tax returns for self-employed
applicants.

**Credit history.** The applicant must have no unresolved defaults recorded
with the Central Credit Register (Biroul de Credit) in the past 24 months.

**Co-borrowers.** A spouse, partner, or family member may be added as a
co-borrower to combine incomes for the eligibility calculation; see the
co-borrower document for the specific rules that apply.

These criteria apply to the Standard Mortgage and the Refinancing Mortgage. The
First-Time Buyer Mortgage modifies the down payment requirement only — see that
product's own document for the reduced percentage and its additional
conditions.
