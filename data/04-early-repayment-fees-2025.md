**Note: this schedule was superseded on 2026-01-15. See the 2026 fee schedule
for current terms.**

For mortgages originated under the 2025 terms, early repayment fees are as
follows.

During the fixed-rate period, an early repayment fee of 1.5 percent of the
remaining principal applies to any prepayment above 20 percent of the
original loan amount in a calendar year. Prepayments up to that 20 percent
threshold are free of charge.

During the variable-rate period, early repayment is free of charge with no
threshold or limit.

Partial prepayments are applied directly to the principal and do not change
the monthly installment unless the customer explicitly requests a
recalculated (lower) installment; by default, the loan term shortens instead.
