title: Mortgage interest rate structure
product: mortgages
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-15

---

Libra Bank mortgages use a two-phase interest rate structure: a fixed
introductory period followed by a variable period for the remainder of the
loan term.

**Fixed period.** The interest rate is fixed for the first 5 years of the
loan, regardless of the total loan term. During this period, the rate does
not change even if market reference rates move.

**Variable period.** After the fixed period ends, the rate becomes variable,
recalculated quarterly as IRCC (the Romanian reference index for consumer
credit) plus a fixed margin set at origination. The margin itself never
changes for the life of the loan; only the IRCC component moves.

**Rate lock.** Customers may request an extended fixed period of 7 or 10
years instead of the standard 5, at a higher starting rate. This must be
selected at application and cannot be changed afterward without a full
refinancing.

**Rate caps.** For loans originated from 2026 onward, the variable-period
rate is capped at a maximum increase of 3 percentage points above the
starting fixed rate, for the life of the loan. Loans originated before 2026
do not carry this cap; see the loan's original contract for its specific
terms.
