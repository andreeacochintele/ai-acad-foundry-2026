---
title: Refinancing an existing mortgage
product: mortgages
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-15
version: 1
---

Customers with an existing mortgage, whether at Moch Bank or another
lender, can apply for a Refinancing Mortgage to obtain a new rate, change the
loan term, or consolidate the remaining balance with other debt.

**Eligibility.** The same eligibility criteria as the Standard Mortgage
apply, except the down payment requirement is replaced by a maximum
refinance LTV of 80 percent of the property's current appraised value.

**Exit fees from the previous lender.** Moch Bank does not cover any early
repayment or exit fee charged by the customer's current lender. The customer
should confirm this cost with their existing bank before applying.

**Consolidation.** Up to 30 percent of the new loan amount may be used to
consolidate other unsecured debts (credit cards, personal loans), provided
the combined debt-service-to-income ratio still meets the 40 percent limit
described in the eligibility criteria.

**Rate reset.** Refinancing restarts the interest rate structure: a new
5-year (or 7- or 10-year) fixed period begins at the refinancing rate then
in effect, regardless of how much of the original fixed period had already
elapsed.

**Timeline.** Refinancing follows the same nine-step application process as
a new mortgage, except the property appraisal step (step 5) may be waived if
an appraisal from the last 12 months is available.
