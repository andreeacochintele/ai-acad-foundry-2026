---
title: Switching rate type mid-term (fixed to variable or back)
product: mortgages
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-15
version: 1
---

A customer already partway through their mortgage can request a one-time
switch of rate type, separate from the rate lock choice made at application.

**How this differs from rate lock.** The interest rate structure document
describes rate lock: choosing a 5, 7, or 10-year fixed period upfront, which
cannot be changed afterward without a full refinancing. A mid-term rate-type
switch is a different mechanism, available once during the life of the loan,
that does not require refinancing.

**From fixed to variable, early.** A customer still inside their fixed period
can request to move onto the variable rate (IRCC plus their existing margin)
before the fixed period ends. This is irreversible for that loan — the
customer cannot switch back to a fixed rate except through a full
refinancing.

**From variable to a new fixed period.** A customer already in the variable
period can request a new fixed period (5, 7, or 10 years) at the rate then
in effect on the loan-to-value rate table for their current LTV. This resets
the early repayment fee schedule's fixed-period clock, since a new fixed
period has effectively begun.

**Timing and fee.** A switch request may only take effect on the loan's
anniversary date (the date the original mortgage was disbursed), and carries
a 150 EUR administrative fee. Requests must be submitted at least 30 days
before the anniversary date.

**Once only.** Only one mid-term rate-type switch is permitted for the life
of a given mortgage; a customer wanting a second change must use refinancing
or portability instead.
