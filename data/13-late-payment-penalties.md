---
title: Late payment penalties
product: mortgages
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-15
version: 1
---

If a monthly mortgage installment is not paid by its due date, the following
penalties apply, in sequence.

**Days 1–5 (grace period).** No penalty applies. The bank sends a courtesy
reminder by SMS and email on day 3.

**Days 6–30.** A late payment penalty of **0.05 percent of the overdue
installment amount per day** applies, calculated from day 6 onward, not from
day 1.

**Day 31 onward.** The loan is reported to the Central Credit Register as
overdue, which affects the customer's credit history for future
applications, including at other banks. The daily penalty from the previous
tier continues to apply.

**Day 91 onward.** The bank may declare the full outstanding loan balance
due immediately (acceleration), rather than only the missed installments,
and may initiate legal recovery proceedings, including foreclosure.

Customers anticipating a missed payment should contact the mortgage
servicing team before the due date; the hardship restructuring document
describes options available before day 1 of a missed payment, which avoid
these penalties entirely.
